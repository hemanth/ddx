CLINICAL METHODOLOGY · QMR-DT BAYESIAN NETWORK, PHRANK INFORMATION CONTENT & SYSTEM ONE
Open Fullscreen Blueprint ↗

Differential synthesizes five decades of peer-reviewed clinical decision support literature - DDXPlus (Fansi Tchango et al., NeurIPS 2022), QMR-DT Bipartite Bayesian Networks (Shwe et al., 1991), Phrank Information Content (Jagadeesh et al., 2019), and Validated Bedside Clinical Decision Rules (HEART, Wells PE, CURB-65, ESI v4) - paired with 10 parallel TypeSafe System One (choice, score, noul) primitives and HIPAA-friendly in-browser OpenJev Qwen3.5-4B WASM inference.

01 DDXPLUS BENCHMARK (NEURIPS 2022)

49 ICD-10 Pathologies & 223 Clinical Findings

Grounded in Fansi Tchango et al. (NeurIPS 2022). Models 110 symptoms and 113 medical antecedents across binary, categorical, and multi-choice attributes (pain localization, radiation, character, and 0–10 intensity) calibrated over N = 8,000 patient encounters.

02 QMR-DT BIPARTITE BAYESIAN (1991)

Positive (LR+) & Pertinent Negative (LR−) Likelihoods

Follows Shwe et al. (1991) reformulation of INTERNIST-1 / QMR-DT: combines demographic priors, positive likelihoods P(e|D), explicit pertinent negative likelihood ratios LR− = (1 − P(e|D)) / (1 − P(e|¬D)), and impossible-finding leakage penalties.

03 PHRANK INFORMATION CONTENT (2019)

Pathognomonic Specificity & Bedside Question Ranking

Implements Jagadeesh et al. (Genetics in Medicine 2019) information-theoretic phenotype ranking: each finding is weighted by IC(e) = −ln P(e) so pathognomonic signs outweigh generic symptoms. Unasked bedside questions are ranked by expected Shannon entropy reduction.

04 SYSTEM ONE & TOP IN-BROWSER QWEN3.5-4B

10 Typed Primitives & #1 In-Browser OpenJev Model

Compiles patient telemetry into 10 parallel System One primitives (4× choice, 2× score, 4× noul) evaluated via TypeSafe Cloud (jev-latest) or the #1 top in-browser OpenJev model (Qwen3.5-4B Q4_K_M GGUF, 84.5% TypeSafe agreement) running locally in WebAssembly.
